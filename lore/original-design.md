# Original occult deck-building design notes

This file proposes original mechanics for the project. It is not a summary of
Lord of the Mysteries (LoTM) or Circle of Inevitability (CoI), and does not claim
that the names, rules, characters, or events below occur in either novel.
No novel quotations or extracted game assets are included.

## Fog: partial knowledge

Fog limits the information shown before a route is chosen. A player may reveal
one enemy intention or one event consequence by spending a temporary resource.
Revealing information changes a decision; it never silently changes combat odds.
The interface should clearly separate a known outcome from an unknown outcome.

Original card proposal: **Pierce the Veil** costs 1 energy, reveals the next enemy
intention, and grants 3 guard. The values are balancing hypotheses, not facts
about an existing game or novel.

## Memory: choices persist across a run

Memory records a chosen benefit and its cost. At rest sites a player may recall
one earlier decision to alter a later event. A visible memory ledger explains
which decision is being used and what will change before the player confirms.
Permanent unlocks should add options rather than require endless stat growth.

Original card proposal: **Borrowed Recollection** costs 1 energy, draws 2 cards,
then puts 1 card from the hand on top of the deck. It is an invented mechanic.

## Cost: power creates an explicit debt

Powerful cards may add a debt counter. Before a threshold is reached, show its
exact effect and how to reduce it. The player can accept short-term danger for
a strong turn without having to guess at a hidden punishment. Costs must have
clear icons, text explanations, and a history available during combat.

Original card proposal: **Unpaid Omen** costs 0 energy, deals 8 damage, and adds
1 debt. Reaching 3 debt adds a temporary burden card at the end of the turn.
These rules belong to this project's design proposal only.

## Ritual: a readable sequence of actions

Ritual rewards completing a visible three-step sequence across one or more turns.
Each step is represented by a card tag and a progress marker. A preview shows
the reward and the reset condition. A broken sequence should be explained in
the combat log so the player can understand the outcome from the interface.

Original card proposal: **Trace the Circle** costs 1 energy and grants 4 guard.
Playing three ritual-tagged cards completes a ritual and draws 1 card. The
sequence and its reward should be tuned through deterministic simulations.

## How to add novel references later

Keep a separate locally supplied .md or .txt source for each authorized summary
or excerpt. Record its title, origin URL, rights, and verification status in the
source manifest. Label speculative interpretations as reference-summary.
Use canon only when an actual primary reference and provenance are supplied;
the local index still reports this as a user-declared claim, not independent
verification. Search results must retain source IDs and line references.
